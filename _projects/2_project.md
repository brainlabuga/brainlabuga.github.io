How is the brain organized in the first weeks of life? Can we predict brain development using measures of neuroplasticity? Do altered trajectories of brain development predict behavior in early childhood? We are working to answer these questions, and many more, in collaboration with colleagues at Washington University in St. Louis. To learn more about the infant neuroimaging data we use in the lab, see the [eLABE study website](https://eedp.wustl.edu/research/elabe-study/). You can also find Dr. Herzberg’s publications using this data set on the Publications page.
