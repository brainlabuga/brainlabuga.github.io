Our brains and bodies work together to coordinate responses to stressful situations. This is good news—we all experience stress every day, whether a new social situation or classroom or an impending deadline at school. In response to these stressful experiences our brains coordinate a series of responses, including changes in brain function and increases in stress hormones, that help us to respond effectively to whatever life throws at us. In this study, we are investigating how the brain responds to events that adolescents experience in school, like giving a speech or doing math problems, and how these brain responses are associated with mental health. The study also collects saliva samples to analyze stress hormone concentrations and a large amount of brain imaging data for cutting-edge individualized network analyses.
