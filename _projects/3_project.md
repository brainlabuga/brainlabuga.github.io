In addition to primary data collection and collaborations across institutions, we leverage publicly available data sets to explore the questions related to lab interests. These studies include the Adolescent Brain Cognitive Development (ABCD) study, the Human Connectome Project, and the Healthy Brain Network data sets. While the interests of lab members vary, all the secondary data analysis projects we conduct are related to the central interests of the lab, including the effects of stress on neurodevelopment, neuroplasticity, and subsequent adaptive or maladaptive behavioral outcomes.
