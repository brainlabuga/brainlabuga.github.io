Welcome to the Brain Research in Adolescence and Infancy (BRAIN) Lab at UGA!

The BRAIN Lab at the University of Georgia, directed by Dr. Max Herzberg, is a developmental cognitive neuroscience lab focused on understanding the impacts of stress on neural and behavioral development. We use multiple neuroimaging techniques, particularly functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI), to measure real-time responses to stressful situations and to develop a deeper understanding of stress-related mental health challenges. We are also exploring innovative methods to track neuroplasticity in ways that may help to optimize the type and timing of interventions aiming to reduce psychopathology risk.
