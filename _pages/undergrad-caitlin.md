<h2 align="left" style="font-weight: normal; font-size: 2em;">Caitlin Upchurch</h2>
Caitlin Upchurch is a sophomore undergraduate student from the metro- Atlanta area, double majoring in neuroscience and communications with a minor in human development & family sciences. In the past she has worked with adolescents volunteering as a mentor in local elementary schools and with ESP. She also has spent her last two years working in an Intensive Care Unit which has given her a strong interest in how past experiences have shaped development and eventually the health outcomes of patients. She is currently hoping to work within healthcare once she graduates, ideally completing some type of graduate school. When not in the lab she is involved with UGA Volunteers Across the World, helping to lead student trips to deliver care to underserved communities, and she loves yoga with her friends.
