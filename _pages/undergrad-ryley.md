<h2 align="left" style="font-weight: normal; font-size: 2em;">Ryley Landers</h2>
Ryley Landers is a sophomore undergraduate student studying Biology and Neuroscience with a Spanish minor at the University of Georgia. In the past, she has worked with children and adolescents by teaching dance and being a summer camp counselor. She intends to become a pediatric doctor specializing in Neurology. Some of her research interests include how early environment shapes development and how stress response is regulated through allostatic processes. Outside of research, she is involved in a dance organization and she loves to read.
